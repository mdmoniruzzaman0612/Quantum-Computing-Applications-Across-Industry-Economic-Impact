# Quantum Computing: Principles, Algorithms, Industrial Applications & Economic Impact

## Overview

This repository contains a university-level study and presentation on **Quantum Computing**, with a particular focus on **Quantum Annealing, D-Wave quantum systems, optimization problems, and the Travelling Salesman Problem (TSP)**.

The project introduces the fundamental principles of quantum computing and then explores how quantum optimization can be applied to complex real-world problems in transportation, logistics, manufacturing, finance, healthcare, and energy.

A major part of the project focuses on converting optimization problems such as TSP into **Quadratic Unconstrained Binary Optimization (QUBO)** models and understanding how these models can be represented using **Ising Hamiltonians** for Quantum Annealing.

The project also discusses the economic potential of quantum technologies, industrial adoption, current technical limitations, and future research directions.
